## Overview
A friendly, deadpan laptop bouncer. Cafe-club warmth in a clear working interface. Name and tagline are proposed brand concepts, not cleared trademarks. The paired eyes are the core mark; assets/mark.svg is the portable master.

## Colors
Grape carries text and primary actions. Lilac carries the protection diagram; citron carries the companion character and supportive tips. Green and amber communicate safe and degraded states with explicit text. The light palette suits daylight cafe use.

## Typography
Bundled Bricolage Grotesque variable font, SIL OFL. Display uses 750, buttons 650, body 400. Headings use tight but readable tracking. Measurement and time figures are tabular. Functional microcopy stays at least 11px.

## Layout
222px sidebar and fluid work area on desktop; top navigation at 850px; one-column content at 600px. Main dashboard leads with protection state, device connection, then session events. Demo controls sit below the work area, visibly separate from product behavior.

## Elevation & Depth
Panels use a single fine border. The phone preview and transient toast use soft offset shadows. No ornamental glow or layered panel shadows.

## Shapes
16px panels, 9px controls. Pills are reserved for concise states. Eyes are crisp, flat geometry with aligned pupils, not a detailed illustrated character.

## Components
Named SVG icons share a 1.7px outline. Interactive controls have visible keyboard focus and 44px minimum height. Selected navigation and simulation controls expose semantic state. The model owns protection invariants; view code renders them. Returning never removes a lock. Pairing and pausing never remove a lock. Reduced motion removes animation. Setup uses explicit discovery and confirmation steps.

## Do's and Don'ts
Put a wink after the fact: “Someone got curious.” Keep errors and recovery literal. Never claim a measured 10m distance or identify a person. Never imply this browser prototype is protecting a real machine. Avoid shame, threats, hidden camera capture, fake customer claims and decorative security jargon.
